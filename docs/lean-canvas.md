# SkillPilot: Lean Canvas

Origin: IBM SkillsBuild for Academia Masterclass 5 deck (aligned with UN SDG 4: Quality Education).

---

## 1. Problem
- AI learning resources are scattered across fragmented platforms (videos, blogs, documentation).
- Learners struggle to stay consistent without structure or daily feedback.
- Learning is not tied directly to practical career goals and hands-on capability.
- Courses quickly go out of date as the AI landscape evolves.
- Online courses have low completion rates because nobody holds learners accountable.
- Students lack mentors for personalized AI career guidance.

**Existing Alternatives:**
YouTube tutorials, Coursera, Udemy, IBM SkillsBuild, ChatGPT, Google Search, LinkedIn Learning.

---

## 2. Customer Segments
- **Primary Segments:** College and engineering students, non-technical learners interested in AI careers, universities, schools, government skilling programs, and CSR-funded education initiatives.
- **Early Adopters:** College AI clubs, students actively preparing for AI career roles, Lenovo Leap participants, IBM SkillsBuild learners, and institutions with dedicated AI/ML labs.

---

## 3. Unique Value Proposition
> "Your personal AI learning companion that learns, teaches, motivates, and evolves every day."

**High-Level Concept:**
Duolingo + ChatGPT + Coursera + personal career coach, specifically built for AI education.

---

## 4. Solution
- **Personalized Learning Route:** Adaptive week-by-week curriculum tailored to student goal, experience level, and study hours.
- **Daily Micro-Lessons:** Clear 15-minute conceptual lessons paired with architecture flowcharts.
- **Hands-on Labs:** Actionable, 15-minute terminal or notebook exercises with runnable code and expected output.
- **Formative Quizzes:** 5-question conceptual comprehension checks with immediate feedback.
- **Progress Tracking:** Local streak count, weekly activity strip, and quiz score tracking.
- **Planned (Roadmap):** Multi-channel study reminders, autonomous content updates.

---

## 5. Channels
- **Acquisition:** College campus drives, student ambassadors, university partnerships, CSR programs, IBM SkillsBuild, Lenovo Leap, LinkedIn, YouTube, Instagram communities.
- **Distribution:** Web application (v1), progressive web app, mobile app, WhatsApp/email notification hooks, and LMS integration.

---

## 6. Revenue Streams
- **B2C:** Freemium access, monthly/annual premium subscriptions, 1-on-1 AI career coaching.
- **B2B:** University and college enterprise licenses, corporate upskilling programs, CSR partnerships, government education initiatives.
- **Additional:** Verified certification exams, portfolio project marketplace, mentor marketplace, sponsored industry courses.

---

## 7. Cost Structure
- **Technology:** LLM API inference costs (Gemini API), edge hosting (Vercel), future database and vector store, messaging APIs (WhatsApp/Email).
- **Development:** AI engineering, full-stack web development, UI/UX design, DevOps.
- **Operations:** Curriculum validation, community support, campus marketing, institutional sales, partnerships.

---

## 8. Key Metrics
- Daily active learners (DAU)
- Route completion rate
- Quiz performance and score progression
- Streak continuity and recovery
- Study reminder engagement rate
- 7 / 30 / 90-day retention
- Career readiness and project portfolio completion
- Net Promoter Score (NPS) and institutional adoption
*(Visible in v1 app: Active streak, week strip, quiz scores, and route progress on the Progress screen).*

---

## 9. Unfair Advantage
- Autonomous agents that keep content current from trusted engineering sources.
- Adaptive personalized routes starting from where the learner actually is.
- Tutor, mentor, and career coach unified into a single coherent interface.
- Multi-channel engagement and direct alignment with UN Sustainable Development Goal 4 (Quality Education).
